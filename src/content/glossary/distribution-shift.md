---
term: Distribution shift
short: >-
  When the data a model sees in use differs from the data it was trained on, for example drawings framed differently from the training images. Test-set accuracy then no longer predicts real-world accuracy.
level: intermediate
aliases: [dataset shift, covariate shift]
