---
term: XOR problem
short: >-
  The observation that a single perceptron cannot compute exclusive OR (output 1 when exactly one of two inputs is 1), because no straight line separates its two classes. A network with one hidden layer solves it.
level: beginner
aliases: [XOR]
related: [perceptron, linear-separability, hidden-layer]

---
Minsky and Papert's 1969 book *Perceptrons* analysed this and related limits of single-layer networks.
