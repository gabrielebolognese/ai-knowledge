---
term: Probability distribution
short: >-
  A rule that gives a probability to every possible outcome, with all the probabilities non-negative and adding up to 1. A classifier's softmax output is a distribution over the classes.
level: beginner
aliases: [distribution]

Example: a forecast of rain 0.6, cloud 0.3 and sun 0.1 is a distribution over three outcomes, because $0.6 + 0.3 + 0.1 = 1$.
