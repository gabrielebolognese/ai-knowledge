---
term: "Cosine similarity"
short: >-
  The cosine of the angle between two vectors: their dot product divided by both lengths. It is 1 for the same direction, 0 for perpendicular and -1 for opposite, whatever the lengths.
level: advanced

Embedding similarity is often measured with cosine rather than the raw dot product, because the dot product also grows with vector length.
