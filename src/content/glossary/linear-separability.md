---
term: Linear separability
short: >-
  Two classes of points are linearly separable when a single straight line (or, in more dimensions, a flat plane) can put all points of one class on one side and all points of the other class on the other side.
level: beginner
aliases: [linearly separable]

The perceptron learning rule is guaranteed to find a separating line when one exists. XOR is the classic example of a pattern that is not linearly separable.
